01 THE INITIATIVE

What is SUTRAM?

SUTRAM is a national platform focused on exploring how Artificial Intelligence can strengthen digital investigation, cyber forensics and incident response.

The hackathon encourages participants to think beyond conventional solutions and develop practical, scalable and technology-driven approaches for challenges in the evolving digital ecosystem.

From intelligent evidence analysis to cyber incident response, participants will have the opportunity to transform innovative ideas into meaningful solutions.
