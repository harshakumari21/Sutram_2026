Host Institution

Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women

Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW) is a women-focused technical university in Delhi committed to education, research, innovation and technology-driven excellence.

Kashmere Gate, Delhi — 110006
IGDTUW Campus • Kashmere Gate, Delhi
About IGDTUW

Education, Innovation & Empowerment

IGDTUW provides an academic environment focused on technical education, research, innovation, entrepreneurship and the advancement of women in technology.

01

Technical Education

Academic programmes cover engineering, technology, applied sciences, architecture and allied professional areas.

02

Research & Innovation

A strong research and innovation ecosystem encourages technology development and interdisciplinary work.

03

Women in Technology

The university promotes opportunities for women to learn, innovate, lead and contribute to emerging technologies.

Academic & Research Focus

Building the Next Generation of Women Technologists

Key areas represented across the university's technical and interdisciplinary ecosystem.

Computer Science & Engineering Information Technology Artificial Intelligence Data Sciences Electronics & Communication Mechanical & Automation Architecture & Planning Applied Sciences Research & Innovation Entrepreneurship
