General Questions

Quick information about SUTRAM 2026

SUTRAM 2026 is an AI-powered cyber investigation and digital forensics hackathon. Participants work on innovation-driven challenges and submit solutions according to the official event workflow and guidelines.
Eligibility and participation requirements will follow the official SUTRAM 2026 guidelines. Please check the Participation section before completing registration.
Registration timelines and important deadlines will be displayed on the SUTRAM 2026 website and participant communication channels. Participants should complete the process before the announced deadline.
The Hackathon Journey section will provide the major stages, activities and flow of SUTRAM 2026.

Registration

Account creation and access

Go to the Registration page, select the appropriate user role and complete the required details. Some roles may require additional verification before access is granted.
If you already have an account, use the Login page instead of creating another account with the same details.
Changes to registration details depend on the event workflow and the status of your registration. Once the participant dashboard is available, permitted changes will be handled according to the official SUTRAM 2026 process.
Use the Forgot Password? option on the Login page. Password recovery will be linked to your registered account email.

Participation & Teams

Questions related to teams and participation

The team workflow and dashboard access will depend on the role assigned during registration. The designated team representative or leader should ensure that required information is submitted correctly and within the announced timelines.
Team composition and eligibility should follow the official SUTRAM 2026 participation guidelines. If multidisciplinary teams are allowed, this will be mentioned in the relevant participation rules.
Any team modification window, if available, will be announced through the participant portal. Once a stage is locked, changes may require organizer approval.

Problem Statements

Understanding the challenges

All published challenges will be available in the Problem Statements section of the SUTRAM 2026 website.
The number of submissions or challenges permitted for each participant or team will be defined in the official SUTRAM 2026 submission guidelines.
Yes. Any official clarification process or communication channel will be announced through the participant portal or organizer communication.

Submission & Evaluation

Project submission and review process

Project submission will be completed through the designated Project Submission workflow or participant dashboard. Required fields, documents and links should be submitted before the deadline.
Evaluation criteria will be presented in the official Evaluation section. Participants should ensure that their solution clearly addresses the problem, demonstrates technical feasibility and follows all submission requirements.
Updates may be allowed only while the submission window remains open. After the deadline, the submitted version may be locked for evaluation.

Technical Support

Login, access and platform issues

First, confirm that you are using the correct registered email and password. If the problem continues, use the password recovery option or contact the official SUTRAM technical support channel.
Try refreshing the page, clearing your browser cache or opening the website in another updated browser. If the issue continues, report the problem with a screenshot and a brief description of what happened.
Use the official contact details provided on the SUTRAM 2026 website. For the fastest resolution, include your registered email, role and a clear description of your query.
